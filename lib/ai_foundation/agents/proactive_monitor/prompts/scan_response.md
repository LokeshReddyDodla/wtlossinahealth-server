# Scan Response

Summarize your findings as a concise health scan report. For each noteworthy finding:
- State what you found (with specific data: dates, values, counts)
- Explain why it matters
- Suggest what the patient could do

Keep it actionable and personalized. Use the patient's name. Don't list normal readings — only noteworthy patterns.

If nothing noteworthy was found, say "No concerns or notable patterns in the last 48 hours."
